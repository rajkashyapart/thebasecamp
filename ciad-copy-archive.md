# PART 3 — lines worth stealing back

Cut for length, not because they were bad. Flag any of these in the brief and
they can come back.

- *"most agencies hand you edited files and move on. nobody's asking if you
  actually made your money back."* — the sharpest line on the old page.
  Falsifiable, non-transferable, and it names the thing the buyer is actually
  afraid of.
- *"the agencies actually good at this are working with brands ten times your
  size. they won't take your call."* — specific, and true in a way a
  competitor can't copy.
- *"the difference isn't ego. it's volume."* — kills the usual pricing-tier
  suspicion in eight words.
- *"you don't need to post more. you need to post smarter."* — clean reframe,
  though "post smarter" is close to a landing-page word.
- *"every client i've ever had came through word of mouth."* — the strongest
  proof he owns. Currently only on About.
- *"you're done with agencies making content like it's 2017."* — the fit check
  in one line.
- *"i'm not advising you on something i don't do myself."*

Weakest of the old lines, for the record: *"built on science"* (can't be
visualised, can't be falsified, and every agency says a version of it) and
*"everything you need to know is in the folders"* (instructions for a UI, not
copy).
