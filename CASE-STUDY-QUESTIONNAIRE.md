## Why I'm asking again — what's actually wrong with the live copy

Not a style problem. Four specific faults, and each one has a question below aimed at it:

1. **Five of the six overviews are the same list of nouns.** "positioning, concept,
   direction, shoot and edit" appears on Kaheen, Fresh Factory, Copper + Cloves and
   Insanely Good Coffee almost verbatim. That's a services list, not a case study.
   → Q3 asks which *one* of those actually did the work, and Q9 asks for the decision.

2. **The insights are transferable.** "honest outperforms perfect", "remote only fails
   when the brief is thin", "niche events sell on atmosphere." Swap the client name and
   every one still reads true — which by Harry Dry's third test means nobody's saying
   anything. → Q7 and Q8.

3. **The outcome restates the metric that's already on screen.** "the work ran 3× their
   average post" sits directly under a box that says 3×. Said twice, so it lands once.
   → Q10 asks what *else* happened.

4. **Nothing is visualisable.** Across all six studies there isn't one object, room,
   date, or sentence somebody said out loud. → Q5, Q6 and Q9 are all fishing for those.

Also outstanding: **Kaheen has three empty sections** (context, insights, problem) and
four of six studies have **no quote**.

## The test before you send it back

Read your answer and ask the three questions you already run copy through:

- **can I picture it?** — a bag of cacao on a table beats "craft chocolate."
- **can it be falsified?** — a number, a date, a count. Not "great results."
- **could nobody else say this?** — if another client's name fits the sentence, it's
  a slogan. Bin it and tell me the awkward specific thing instead.

Three yeses and I can write the study. Three nos and I'll write another aphorism.

*(Reusable — this is the block. Six filled copies follow for the current studies, and

### A · The facts

1. **★ What do they sell, and to whom?** One line, the way you'd say it to a friend
   who's never heard of them. Not their tagline.
   →

2. **★ What you actually did, and the counts.** Videos, photos, shoot days, over what
   months. Which of positioning / ideation / concept / direction / shoot / edit /
   colour / distribution were yours, and which one carried the job.
   →

3. **How they found you** — referral, DM, someone saw a video. If you still have the
   first message, paste it.
   →

4. **What it cost them.** (private) Doesn't go on the page. It tells me what was at
   stake, which changes how the study is written.
   →

### B · Before you

5. **★ What were they posting before?** Describe one actual post of theirs and why it
   wasn't landing. "Nothing" is a valid answer — say so.
   →

6. **What they asked for vs. what they needed.** Their words for the ask, and how the
   real problem turned out to be different.
   →

### C · The thinking — *this is the section that decides if the page is worth reading*

7. **★ What did you notice about THIS brand, category or audience that you couldn't
   have guessed before you started?** Not a principle. The thing you learned on site.
   →

8. **What did you try that didn't work — or what did you two disagree about?** The
   argument is usually where the insight is. If you overrode them and were right, say
   what you overrode.
   →

9. **The one decision that made the difference.** The call another editor wouldn't have
   made. And if there's an object, place or habit in the footage that ended up carrying
   the whole thing — name it.
   →

### D · The result

10. **★ What happened, other than the multiple?** DMs, inbound, a hire, a sellout, a
    reorder, a follow-on month, they stopped needing you. The multiple is already on
    the page; I need the thing it caused.
    →

11. **Where the multiple comes from.** (private) Their analytics or yours, over what
    window, against what baseline. I won't publish it — I just need to not be lying,
    and to hold the hedge where you put it ("up to 5×" stays "up to 5×").
    →

### E · Loose ends

12. **Best single video from the set**, and roughly what it did.
    →
13. **Quote** — do you have one, or can you ask? Name + role + a yes to publishing.
    →
14. **One line about them as a person** — what they're like to work with.
    →
