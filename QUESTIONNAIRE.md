# INTAKE QUESTIONNAIRE — rajkashyap.studio

Fill what you can; skip what doesn't apply. **Be specific and raw — numbers, names, real sentences. Don't write "marketing copy," I'll shape the voice.** Vague answers make a vague site; specific answers make a site that closes cold leads. One honest number beats a paragraph of adjectives.

Answer right under each prompt (a line or two is plenty unless it says otherwise). Where you can't share something publicly, write **(private)** and I'll keep it off the site.

> The lens: imagine someone sharp and skeptical — who hates fluff and only respects evidence, leverage, and clear thinking — is deciding whether to hire you. Every answer is you earning that yes.

## PART 1 — POSITIONING (the sharp stuff)
*Powers: homepage, Work With Me, the About pro-summary, Content in a Day.*

1. **One sentence:** what do you do, and for whom? (no jargon)
   →

2. **The transformation.** Before working with you a client is ______ ; after, they are ______.
   →

3. **Specific knowledge.** What do you understand about content / attention / why people stop scrolling that most creators and agencies *don't*? Be concrete — a real principle, not "we tell stories."
   →

4. **Unfair advantage.** Why can't a client just hire a cheaper editor or a bigger agency and get the same thing? What about you is genuinely hard to copy?
   →

5. **Contrarian belief.** What does most of your industry get *wrong* about content?
   →

6. **The mechanism / leverage.** Explain, to a skeptic, how *one shoot day* becomes a month of content that performs. What's the actual system that makes that possible (and not just "batching")?
   →

7. **Accountability.** What outcome do you put your name on? How do you know if you've *failed* a client?
   →

8. **Definition of a win.** How do you define success for a client — in *their* terms (money, leads, inbound, authority), not views?
   →

9. **Who this is NOT for.** When do you turn someone down? What kind of client is a bad fit?
   →

10. **The one thing.** If a client remembers exactly one thing about working with you, what should it be?
